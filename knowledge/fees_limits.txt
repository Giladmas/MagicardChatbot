MAGICARD — FEES AND LIMITS

Magicard may apply fees to operations such as card creation, card funding, withdrawals, or other transactions.

Transaction limits may also apply.

Fees and limits can change and should not be guessed.

If the current fee or limit is not explicitly available in the chatbot's knowledge base, the chatbot must not invent a value.

The customer should check the current amount displayed by Magicard before confirming a transaction or contact Magicard support for clarification.
