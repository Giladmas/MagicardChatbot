MAGICARD — AML & KYC POLICY

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Magicard applies Anti-Money-Laundering (AML), Know-Your-Customer (KYC), counter-terrorist-financing (CTF), sanctions, and related compliance controls designed to prevent the Services from being used for financial crime.

The AML & KYC Policy applies to all Users of the Services.

Magicard's controls are designed to deter, detect, and report suspicious activity and to verify the identity of its Users.

Before granting access to certain features, and on an ongoing basis, Magicard may require Users to provide identifying information and documentation.

Required information may include full legal name, date of birth, nationality, residential address, a government-issued identification document, and proof of address.

Magicard verifies information using internal and third-party tools.

Magicard may request additional information or repeat verification at any time.

Customers must provide truthful and accurate information and cooperate with verification requests.

Magicard may apply Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) when a User, Transaction, or relationship presents a higher risk.

Higher-risk situations may include higher transaction volumes, unusual transaction patterns, or connections to higher-risk jurisdictions.

Enhanced Due Diligence may include requesting source-of-funds or source-of-wealth information.

Magicard may require senior-management approval to continue a higher-risk relationship.

Magicard applies additional scrutiny to Users who are politically exposed persons (PEPs), or who are connected to politically exposed persons, in accordance with applicable requirements.

Magicard monitors activity on a risk-sensitive basis to identify unusual or suspicious patterns.

As part of compliance monitoring, Magicard may apply limits, request explanations, place holds on Transactions, delay Transactions, or decline Transactions.

Magicard may suspend or close Accounts where necessary to meet its compliance obligations.

Magicard screens Users and Transactions against applicable sanctions and watch lists.

Magicard does not provide Services to persons subject to applicable sanctions or persons located in restricted jurisdictions.

Magicard may block or reject Transactions involving sanctioned persons or restricted jurisdictions.

Magicard Services must not be used for money laundering, terrorist financing, fraud, corruption, sanctions evasion, or any other unlawful activity.

Accounts connected to prohibited activity may be frozen or closed and may be reported to relevant authorities.

Where required by law, Magicard reports suspicious activity to competent authorities.

Magicard may be legally prohibited from telling a customer that a suspicious activity report has been or may be made.

Magicard may also be required to take actions such as freezing funds as a result of its legal obligations.

Magicard retains identification, verification, and transaction records for the periods required by applicable law.

By using the Services, Users agree to cooperate with Magicard's verification, monitoring, and compliance requests.

Users must provide truthful and accurate information.

Failure to cooperate with compliance requests may result in limitations, suspension, or closure of an Account.

Attempts to use Magicard for prohibited purposes may result in limitations, suspension, closure of the Account, and, where appropriate, reporting to authorities.
