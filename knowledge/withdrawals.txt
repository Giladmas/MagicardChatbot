MAGICARD — WITHDRAWALS

Customers can withdraw funds from their Magicard USD wallet as USDT using the TRC20 network.

The customer provides a valid TRC20 wallet address and the withdrawal amount.

A withdrawal request may require approval before the USDT is sent.

Customers should carefully verify their destination wallet address before confirming a withdrawal.

Withdrawal limits: the minimum withdrawal is $10 and the maximum is $100,000 per transaction, with a fee of 1% + $1. Customers can view their current limits under Tariffs & Limits in Account, at the bottom right of the home screen.

Blockchain transactions cannot normally be reversed after they are sent.

If a withdrawal remains pending or fails, the customer should contact Magicard support.
