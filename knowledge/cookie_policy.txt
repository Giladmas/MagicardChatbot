MAGICARD — COOKIE POLICY

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Magicard uses cookies and similar technologies on its website to operate, secure, personalize, analyze, and improve the Services.

Cookies are small text files placed on a customer's device when visiting a website.

Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, and software development kits (SDKs).

These technologies can allow a website to recognize a device, remember preferences, keep a customer signed in, and understand how the website is used.

Strictly necessary cookies are required for the Magicard website and customer Accounts to function.

Strictly necessary cookies may be used for security, authentication, and session management.

Strictly necessary cookies cannot be switched off in Magicard's systems.

Preference cookies remember customer choices such as language and display settings to provide a more personalized experience.

Performance and analytics cookies help Magicard understand how visitors interact with the website so that performance can be measured and improved.

Functional cookies enable enhanced functionality and features.

Magicard uses cookies to operate and secure the Services.

Cookies may be used to authenticate Users and help prevent fraud.

Cookies may be used to remember customer settings.

Cookies may be used to analyze and improve website performance and usability.

Some cookies may be set by third-party services that appear on Magicard pages, such as analytics or security providers.

Third parties may process information according to their own privacy and cookie policies.

Customers can control and delete cookies through their browser settings.

Most browsers allow customers to refuse or remove cookies.

Disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect Magicard functionality, including the ability to sign in and complete Transactions.

Magicard may update its Cookie Policy from time to time.

An updated version will be posted with a revised date.

For questions about Magicard's use of cookies, customers should contact Magicard through the Support section of the Magicard website.
