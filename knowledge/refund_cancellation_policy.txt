MAGICARD — REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY
Last updated: June 10, 2026

Most processed Transactions, including deposits, transfers, withdrawals and card payments, are final and may be irreversible. Customers should review Transaction details before confirming.

Fees for completed Services and Transactions are non-refundable except where required by law or where a Transaction failed because of a verifiable technical error attributable to Magicard. Applicable fees are displayed at the time of the Transaction.

If a Transaction fails, is duplicated, or appears erroneous, contact Magicard through Support as soon as possible and within any timeframe required by law or network rules. Magicard will investigate. Where a refund or correction is due, the amount will be credited to the Wallet or original funding source within a reasonable period, subject to provider and network timelines.

Card transactions are processed through third-party issuers and payment networks and are subject to their dispute and chargeback rules and timeframes. Magicard provides reasonable assistance with legitimate disputes, but the relevant provider or network determines the outcome.

If a Transaction has not yet been processed, a customer may request cancellation through the Service. Magicard will make reasonable efforts to action the request but cannot guarantee cancellation once a Transaction has been initiated.

When an Account is closed, any remaining eligible balance will be made available according to applicable law after outstanding fees or obligations are deducted and subject to required verification and legal checks.

Refund and settlement times depend on the relevant payment method, provider and network. Magicard processes amounts due without undue delay once the applicable conditions are met.

For refund, cancellation or dispute requests, contact Magicard through the Support section and provide the relevant Transaction details.
