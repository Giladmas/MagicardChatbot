MAGICARD — KYC

Magicard may require customers to complete identity verification (KYC).

Customers must provide accurate personal information when completing verification.

The required information or documents depend on the Magicard verification process presented to the customer.

Submitted information must belong to the customer completing the verification.

Customers should not send identity documents through the chatbot.

If KYC verification fails or remains pending, the customer should contact Magicard support.
