MAGICARD — APP NAVIGATION GUIDE

ACCOUNT
The Account section is located at the bottom right of the home screen.

Under Account, customers can access:
- Account Info
- Tariffs & Limits
- Transactions
- 2FA Security
- Get Support
- Language
- Dark Mode
- Light Mode
- Change Password
- Delete Account
- Logout
- Legal & Policies

CARDS
Card information is located under the Cards icon at the bottom of the home screen.

WALLET
The Wallet section is located at the bottom left of the home screen.
Customers can see their latest transactions and Wallet overview in the Wallet section.
