MAGICARD — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Magicard collects, uses, discloses, stores, and protects personal information when customers access or use its website and services.

Magicard may collect identity and contact information including name, date of birth, nationality, email address, telephone number, and residential address.

Magicard may collect verification information including government-issued identification documents, proof of address, photographs or selfies, and other information required for KYC and AML purposes.

Magicard may collect financial and transactional information including wallet balances, deposits, transfers, withdrawals, card details, and transaction history.

Magicard may collect technical and usage information including IP address, device and browser information, operating system, log data, and information about how customers interact with the Services.

Magicard may retain records of communications between customers and the support team.

Information may be collected directly from customers when they register, verify their identity, or contact support.

Information may also be collected automatically through use of the Services, including through cookies and similar technologies.

Magicard may receive information from third parties such as identity-verification, fraud-prevention, and payment providers.

Magicard may use personal information to create and administer customer Accounts.

Information may be used to provide, operate, and maintain Wallet and Card services.

Information may be used to verify customer identity and comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including AML/KYC requirements, sanctions screening, and reporting.

Information may be used to process and monitor Transactions.

Information may be used to detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents.

Information may be used to provide customer support and communicate with customers about the Services.

Information may be used to improve, personalize, and secure the Services.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, Magicard may rely on performance of its contract with the customer, compliance with legal obligations, legitimate interests such as security, fraud prevention, and improving the Services, and customer consent where required.

Magicard may share information with service providers and partners that help operate the Services, including card issuers, payment processors, identity-verification providers, fraud-prevention providers, cloud-hosting providers, and communications providers.

Magicard may share information with regulators, law-enforcement bodies, courts, and other authorities where required by law or where necessary to protect its rights.

Information may also be shared with parties involved in a corporate transaction such as a merger or acquisition.

Magicard does not sell customers' personal information.

Customer information may be processed or stored in countries other than the customer's own country.

Where personal data is transferred internationally, Magicard takes steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable law.

Magicard retains customer information while an Account is active and afterward for periods required by legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, and agreement-enforcement obligations.

Magicard implements technical and organizational measures designed to protect customer information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Security measures include encryption in transit, access controls, and monitoring.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and Magicard cannot guarantee absolute security.

Subject to applicable law, customers may have the right to access personal data held about them.

Customers may have the right to request correction of inaccurate data.

Customers may have the right to request deletion of their data.

Customers may have the right to object to or restrict certain processing.

Customers may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Customers may have the right to request portability of certain data.

Some rights may be limited where Magicard is required to retain information for legal or regulatory reasons.

Customers should contact Magicard through the Support section of the Service to exercise privacy rights.

Magicard uses cookies and similar technologies to operate, secure, and improve the Services.

More information about cookies is provided in the Magicard Cookie Policy.

Magicard Services are not directed to and may not be used by persons under 18 years old.

Magicard does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Magicard Services may contain links to third-party websites or services.

Magicard's Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites or services, and customers should review the relevant third party's privacy practices.

Magicard may update its Privacy Policy from time to time.

An updated version will be posted with a revised Last updated date and becomes effective when posted.

For privacy-related questions or requests, customers should contact Magicard through the Support section of the Magicard website.
