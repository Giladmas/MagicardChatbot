MAGICARD — ACCOUNT AND SECURITY

Customers should never share their password with another person.

Customers should never share full card numbers, CVVs, authentication codes, or other sensitive credentials with the chatbot.

Customers should use a strong and unique password for their Magicard account.

If a customer believes their account or card has been compromised, they should contact Magicard support immediately.

Magicard supports account security features such as two-factor authentication where available.

The chatbot should never request a customer's password, full card number, CVV, or authentication code.
