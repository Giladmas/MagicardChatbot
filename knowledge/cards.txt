MAGICARD — VIRTUAL CARDS

Magicard allows customers to create virtual Mastercard cards.

Virtual cards are funded using the customer's Magicard USD wallet balance.

Customers must have sufficient wallet balance to cover the card funding amount and any applicable fees.

Customers can add funds to an existing supported virtual card.

Unused card balance can be withdrawn from the card back to the customer's Magicard wallet.

Cards can be used for supported online purchases.

Some merchants may decline virtual or prepaid cards according to their own policies.

Card details such as the card number, expiry date, and CVV are sensitive information and should never be shared with another person.

If a card transaction is unexpectedly declined, the customer should verify their card status and available balance before contacting Magicard support.

To create a virtual card, the customer goes to the Cards section (found under the Cards icon at the bottom of the home screen) and starts the card creation flow there. Sufficient wallet balance is needed to cover the card creation amount plus the applicable fee before the card can be created.

Each customer can have a maximum of 3 active virtual cards at a time. Once this limit is reached, no additional cards can be created.

Customers must complete identity verification (KYC) before they can create a virtual card.

If creating a virtual card fails, the most common reasons are: identity verification (KYC) has not been completed; the wallet balance does not cover the card creation amount plus the fee (3% + $3); the card creation amount is below the $10 minimum; or the customer already has the maximum of 3 active cards. The customer should check these, then try again. If it still fails, contact Magicard support.
