MAGICARD — VIRTUAL CARDS

Magicard allows customers to create virtual Mastercard cards.

Virtual cards are funded using the customer's Magicard USD wallet balance.

Customers must have sufficient wallet balance to cover the card funding amount and any applicable fees.

Customers can add funds to an existing supported virtual card.

Unused card balance can be withdrawn from the card back to the customer's Magicard wallet.

Cards can be used for supported online purchases.

Some merchants may decline virtual or prepaid cards according to their own policies.

Card details such as the card number, expiry date, and CVV are sensitive information and should never be shared with another person.

If a card transaction is unexpectedly declined, the customer should verify their card status and available balance before contacting Magicard support.
