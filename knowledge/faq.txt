MAGICARD — GENERAL FAQ

Q: What is Magicard?
A: Magicard is a service that allows customers to manage a USD wallet, deposit USDT, create virtual Mastercard cards, and use those cards for online payments.

Q: What currency does my Magicard wallet use?
A: The Magicard wallet uses USD.

Q: What can I do with Magicard?
A: You can deposit USDT, maintain a USD wallet balance, create virtual cards, fund your cards, spend online, withdraw unused card funds back to your wallet, and withdraw wallet funds as USDT.

Q: Where can I get help if the chatbot cannot answer my question?
A: Please contact Magicard customer support.
